# Sprint-4-Final-Project


Executive Summary
The business has processed 99,441 orders, showing a substantial customer base and meaningful sales activity. However, monthly order trends show a peak in November 2017, followed by a noticeable decline after August 2018. This change is worth investigating to determine whether it was caused by lower customer demand, marketing activity, or other business factors. Understanding the order-status breakdown will also help leadership evaluate overall business health and identify opportunities to improve order fulfillment.

Product performance shows that Bed, Bath & Table was the strongest category by products sold, while Telephony was the lowest performer among the categories analyzed. Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue, with an average review score of 4.142. Interestingly, the category Cool Stuff had the highest average review score at 4.146, despite ranking eighth out of the ten categories in revenue. This suggests an opportunity to explore whether highly rated products in lower-revenue categories could benefit from increased promotion.

Customer orders are heavily concentrated in São Paulo (SP), which accounted for 41,746 orders, or 41.9% of the total. This creates an opportunity to strengthen the company's presence in other regions through targeted marketing and customer outreach. My recommendation is that leadership investigate the decline in monthly orders after August 2018 while prioritizing marketing efforts in regions outside São Paulo. At the same time, the company should continue investing in high-revenue categories such as Health & Beauty and explore ways to increase sales of well-reviewed products in lower-performing categories.
